Voynich: compare the actual letterforms

Experiment 063. These five cases come from the user-supplied 2025 ZL EVA transcription. EVA is a transcription notation, not a decipherment. Zoom and drag each photograph to inspect the cited line. The scan images load from a public source; internet is required. The colored 2014 Yale scans remain preferable for decisive paleographic work.

Important: This viewer does not pre-mark individual glyph boundaries. Location labels refer to transcription lines. The visually tempting m = iin hypothesis remains unproved; the controls show many other same-stem alternative endings.
