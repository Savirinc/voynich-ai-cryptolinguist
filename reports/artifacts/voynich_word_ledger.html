Voynich Word Ledger

Every distinct word in the Voynich manuscript's working pages, with how often it occurs and where. Words are in EVA, the standard Latin-letter transliteration of the unknown script. The 15 leaves held back for the project's final test are left out.

Count is occurrences across all working pages; Pages is how many pages the word appears on. A / B are the two writing varieties identified by Prescott Currier (A mostly early herbal and pharmacy pages; B the bathing, star and later herbal pages). Section columns show where the word occurs by type of illustration. Source: ZL 3b transcription, clean tokens only.
