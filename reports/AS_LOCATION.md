**Dispersion over 152 pages:** p = 0.95, no excess clustering. The 10% of pages with the most repeats hold 29% of
them, against 32% expected by chance.

**Verdict (pre-registered): WIDESPREAD.** The lack of avoidance of identical neighbours is spread evenly:
- across all three main scribes;
- across sections (herbal A, herbal B, the biological quire M, the stars/recipes quire T);
- across pages.

It is not a local list or label effect. It is a property of how the whole text was written.

The single exception, quire O (AS 0.35), rests on very few expected repeats. It is noted, not interpreted.

## Consequence
The informal lean recorded after E045 stands. C21 is a whole-book property that ordinary meaningful writing does not
show, and that non-meaningful writing (people writing gibberish, the copy generator) approaches. Within "real
language", only languages with doubling grammar reach it.
